WiFi Speaker

Stream audio over your local network to any device with a 3.5 mm output

Use the same room code on both the sender and receiver device.

🎵 Send Audio Stream from this device 🔊 Receive Audio Play on this device's 3.5 mm output

How it works

  1. Plug your external speaker into the receiver device's 3.5 mm jack.
  2. Open this page on both devices (they must be on the same network).
  3. Enter the same room code, then choose Send on one and Receive on the other.
  4. On the sender, pick an audio source (mic, tab audio, or screen share) and start streaming.
